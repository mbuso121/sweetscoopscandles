Our story

Made to Create a Feeling

Shade’s Sweet Scoops Candles started as a school business idea a way to turn a love of dessert-inspired design into something real. Every candle is still hand-poured, hand-piped and hand-finished in small batches.

We wanted candles that did more than sit on a shelf. Each scoop, swirl and topper is designed to make someone smile, whether it’s a gift, a treat, or the finishing touch on a table you’re proud of.
